## 🌟 Top Medical Imaging AI Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Radiology AI, Diagnostic Imaging Analysis, Triage, Segmentation, Detection & Clinical Workflow Integration*  

**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Medical Imaging AI**. These tools apply deep learning to CT, MRI, X-ray, and other modalities for detection, triage, segmentation, quantification, and decision support, helping radiologists and clinicians improve speed, accuracy, and prioritization of critical findings. This is a comprehensive, SEO-friendly guide to the best radiology AI tools available today.

**Examples** include Aidoc, Arterys, Viz.ai, Subtle Medical, Qure.ai, Lunit, Nanox AI, Brainomix, Flywheel, Blackford Platform, Zebra Medical Vision, Gleamer, Oxipit, Rad AI, and Deepc (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for medical image AI frameworks, multi-organ segmentation, annotation tools, model deployment, and related open platforms — ideal for researchers, hospitals, and developers seeking transparent, standards-based alternatives or complementary components to commercial medical imaging AI solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## 💻 Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Project MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI)** <a href="https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Project-MONAI/MONAI?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  The leading open-source PyTorch-based framework for medical imaging AI, covering training, intelligent annotation, and deployment.

- **[nnU-Net](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet)** <a href="https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Self-configuring deep learning framework for biomedical image segmentation that remains a strong baseline and foundation for many medical AI models.

- **[OHIF Viewer](https://github.com/OHIF/Viewers)** <a href="https://github.com/OHIF/Viewers/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OHIF/Viewers?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Open-source, web-based DICOM viewer that supports AI overlays, segmentation, and integration with modern medical imaging workflows.

- **[3D Slicer](https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer)** <a href="https://github.com/Slicer/Slicer/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Slicer/Slicer?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Powerful open-source platform for medical image visualization, analysis, and AI integration, extensively used in research and clinical prototyping.

- **[ITK](https://github.com/InsightSoftwareConsortium/ITK)** <a href="https://github.com/InsightSoftwareConsortium/ITK/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/InsightSoftwareConsortium/ITK?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Foundational open-source toolkit for medical image processing, registration, and analysis that underpins many higher-level AI pipelines.

- **[MedSAM](https://github.com/bowang-lab/MedSAM)** <a href="https://github.com/bowang-lab/MedSAM/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bowang-lab/MedSAM?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Open-source adaptations of the Segment Anything Model family tailored for 2D and 3D medical image segmentation.

- **[TotalSegmentator](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator)** <a href="https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/wasserth/TotalSegmentator?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Widely used open-source multi-organ segmentation model that segments 100+ anatomical structures from CT with high accuracy.

- **[MedicalNet](https://github.com/Tencent/MedicalNet)** <a href="https://github.com/Tencent/MedicalNet/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Tencent/MedicalNet?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  A collection of 3D ResNet models pre-trained on diverse medical datasets to accelerate 3D medical image analysis.

- **[TorchIO](https://github.com/fepegar/torchio)** <a href="https://github.com/fepegar/torchio/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/fepegar/torchio?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Open-source library for loading, preprocessing, augmentation, and patch-based sampling of 3D medical images in PyTorch workflows.

- **[TorchXRayVision](https://github.com/mlmed/torchxrayvision)** <a href="https://github.com/mlmed/torchxrayvision/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mlmed/torchxrayvision?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  An open-source library for working with chest X-ray datasets and pre-trained deep learning models.

- **[FastSurfer](https://github.com/Deep-MI/FastSurfer)** <a href="https://github.com/Deep-MI/FastSurfer/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Deep-MI/FastSurfer?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  A fast and highly accurate deep-learning based neuroimaging pipeline for structural MRI.

- **[DeepLesion](https://github.com/rsummers11/CADLab)** <a href="https://github.com/rsummers11/CADLab/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/rsummers11/CADLab?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  A large-scale dataset and tools for universal lesion detection in CT images.

- **[MONAI Label](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAILabel)** <a href="https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAILabel/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Project-MONAI/MONAILabel?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Intelligent open-source annotation tool with active learning that integrates with 3D Slicer, OHIF, and other viewers for efficient dataset creation.

- **[MONAI Model Zoo](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo)** <a href="https://github.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo/stargazers"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Project-MONAI/model-zoo?style=social&color=white" alt="stars"/></a>  
  Collection of ready-to-use, community-contributed medical imaging models packaged in the MONAI Bundle format.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **End-to-end framework**: MONAI (Core + Label + Deploy) is the most comprehensive open ecosystem for medical imaging AI.
- **Ready-to-run models**: TotalSegmentator and the MONAI Model Zoo provide high-quality segmentation out of the box.
- **Visualization & annotation**: 3D Slicer and OHIF remain the standard open platforms for interactive work.
- **Foundational libraries**: nnU-Net, ITK, and TorchIO power countless research and production pipelines.
- Many specialized **radiology AI**, **pathology AI**, and **multi-modal** open-source projects continue to appear on GitHub.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **MONAI** for training and deployment, **TotalSegmentator or MONAI Model Zoo** models for segmentation, **3D Slicer / OHIF** for visualization and annotation, and open DICOM infrastructure to create a complete research or prototype medical imaging AI platform.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Medical imaging AI systems are regulated medical devices in most jurisdictions; clinical use requires appropriate regulatory clearance (FDA, CE, etc.), validation, and integration with clinical workflows.
- Self-hosted open-source solutions are primarily suited for research, education, and development; production clinical deployment demands rigorous quality management, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance.
